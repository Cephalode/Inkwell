## Variant: Focused Action

### Design stance
Eliminate decision fatigue. The dashboard answers one question: "What should I do right now?" — then shows supporting context below.

### Key choices
- Layout: Single column, centered, max-w-4xl — no sidebar, no navigation noise
- Typography: Bold "What would you like to study?" as the hero question
- Color: Teal accent for primary (continue), amber for secondary (flashcards), muted for tertiary (AI chat)
- Interaction: Three large action cards at the top with click feedback, activity feed at bottom

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Getting users into studying immediately, low cognitive load
- Weak at: Less information density, no courses/subjects visible, requires scrolling for details

### Best for
Users who want to open the app and start working immediately. The "continue reading" card with its progress bar is the star.
