## Variant 007 — Desk Broadsheet

### Design stance
The home page as the front page of a daily student newspaper: masthead, section nav, a lead story (your next task), briefs column, courses as a section below the fold, classifieds-style document lists.

### Key choices
- Layout: 3-column broadsheet — lead story (2 cols) + Briefs rail, then Courses (3-up with rules), then "Recent Acquisitions" wire columns + Notices, then "The Margin" (highlights/quotes)
- Typography: Source Serif 4 throughout, mono for meta lines; uppercase letterspaced kickers
- Color: Broadsheet tokens only; cyan accent for kickers/progress, pink for flags
- Interaction: lead story cycles (Continue reading → due cards → course step), Briefs Today/Week toggle, dark/light toggle
- Density: highest of the three — everything visible on one long page

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: sense of daily ritual, information richness, feels like "the app has a life"
- Weak at: heaviest to build; monospace noise can fight the serif; risky on mobile (needs responsive collapse)

### Best for
Daily-driver users who want everything at a glance and enjoy the newspaper metaphor.
