## Variant 008 — Quiet Focus

### Design stance
Anti-dashboard. One sentence telling you the single best thing to do next, one Begin button; everything else is a quiet "after that" checklist. Modeled on distraction-free writing tools (iA Writer, calm reading apps).

### Key choices
- Layout: single narrow column (760px), content starts ~16vh down — lots of air
- Typography: Source Serif 4 only (no mono), oversized statement headline with italic emphasis
- Color: almost monochrome; cyan appears only on Begin and hover states
- Interaction: "not this — something else" cycles the hero through 4 real task types; "After that" tasks check off on click
- Density: lowest of the three

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: zero decision fatigue; the page always answers "what now?"; trivial to build; gorgeous on mobile
- Weak at: no at-a-glance overview of courses/deadlines (deliberately delegated to other pages); power users may find it sparse

### Best for
Users who open Inkwell to study, not to manage studying. Pairs well with the existing narrow "Today" page — this is its confident, opinionated successor.
